# wrong. It was checked three ways rather than assumed:
#
#   1. The original reads NO incoming register. Its first instruction is
#      `MOVE.W _ESQ_GlobalTickCounter,D0`, a global load. A1/A5/A6 are ignored.
#   2. The original saves NOTHING on entry and touches only D0/D1/A0/A1, which
